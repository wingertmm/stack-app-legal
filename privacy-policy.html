Stack App — Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.stackapp.protocol · Effective Date: 2026-11-13

1. Local-Only Storage (No Cloud Collection)

All supplement definitions, dose logs, adherence history, inventory counts, and home-screen widget snapshots remain 100% locally on this device using on-device SQLite and system widget storage. Stack App does not require an account, does not contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes zero background network uploads.

2. User-Initiated Data Sharing

Data leaves your device only if you explicitly select Share Text Summary on the Export screen to pass a summary to an app of your choice through your device’s native share sheet. You can modify or delete any stored supplement directly within the app or remove all local data by uninstalling Stack App.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • Notifications (iOS & Android 13+): Used solely to deliver your scheduled supplement window alerts and quick-action buttons (Mark Taken, Snooze 30m, Skip).
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android 12+/14+): Optional OS permission used solely so timed supplement windows and mineral-spacing delays fire at the exact user-configured minute without battery-batching drift.
