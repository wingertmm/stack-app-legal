Stack App — Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.stackapp.protocol · Effective Date: 2026-11-20

1. Local-First Core Storage (Free Tier = 100% Offline)

All personal supplement definitions, intake dose logs, adherence streaks, daily hydration and protein habit logs, personal biomarker records, inventory counts, cached reference entries, and home-screen widget snapshots are stored 100% locally on this device using on-device SQLite (stack_app.db) and system widget storage. Stack App does not require an account, contains zero third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, makes zero outbound network requests on the Free tier, never initializes billing SDKs on the Free tier, and never uploads personal health logs, inventory, or biomarker histories in the background.

2. User-Initiated Exports & Owner-Scoped Crew Shares

Protocol summaries leave your device only when you explicitly initiate an action:

  • Native OS Share Sheet: Tapping Share Text Summary under the Share tab or Share List under the Stack tab opens your device's system share sheet so you control the destination app.
  • Opt-In Buddy / Crew Shares (Paid): When explicitly shared under the Share tab, moderated shares include only your display alias, compound names, dosages, and optional adherence/streak metrics—never biomarkers, pricing, inventory, or personal notes. When synced to our server, your pseudonymous owner_sub token claim is stored solely server-side to authorize owner-only deletion (DELETE /v1/social/shares/:shareId) and is omitted from public group leaderboards.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Camera Processing

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver your scheduled supplement window alerts and quick-action buttons (Mark Done, Snooze 30m, Skip).
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android 12+/14+): Optional OS permission used solely so timed supplement windows and mineral-spacing delays fire at the exact user-configured minute without battery-batching drift.
  • Camera (CAMERA on Android / NSCameraUsageDescription on iOS): Used exclusively when you open the viewfinder inside Add Supplement to scan a bottle UPC barcode or read supplement label text via on-device OCR (Google ML Kit on Android / Apple Vision on iOS). Label photos are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded by default. Microphone and external storage permissions (RECORD_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) are explicitly blocked and never requested.

4. Subscriptions, Billing & Optional Paid HTTPS Services

If you choose to subscribe or restore a Paid purchase:

  • Verified Store Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING / StoreKit via RevenueCat): The RevenueCat billing SDK initializes only when you explicitly tap Unlock Paid Tier or Restore Purchases. Store purchase tokens and a pseudonymous app_user_id are transmitted over HTTPS to verify your active entitlement and issue short-lived, 15-minute signed HS256 JWTs.
  • Paid HTTPS Knowledge & UPC Lookups: Release builds enforce strict TLS (https://) for curated supplement catalog delta sync (ETag), human-reviewed research digests, recall notices, and opt-in UPC catalog lookups on local cache miss (cached locally in paid_upc_cache_v1 so repeat scans stay offline). Personal biomarkers, intake logs, inventory, and pricing remain strictly on-device.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Controls

You may edit or delete any supplement, habit, or biomarker entry directly in Stack App, revoke any shared Paid crew snapshot anytime via Revoke Share (DELETE /v1/social/shares/:shareId), cancel Paid subscriptions in Google Play or Apple App Store account settings, or uninstall Stack App to remove all local SQLite data permanently.
